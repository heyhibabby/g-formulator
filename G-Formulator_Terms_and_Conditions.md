## 3. Description of Services

G-Formulator is a professional device-based tool for formulating, balancing, optimizing, and scaling gelato, ice cream, sorbet, plant-based frozen desserts, and related specialties. Its functions include:

- An ingredient database (approximately 380 built-in ingredients) with compositional data (sugars, fats, milk solids-not-fat, other solids, water, protein, POD/PAC-type sweetening and anti-freezing values), plus your own custom and edited ingredient entries and CSV import/export of the database;
- A **1-Click Multi-Recipe Engine**: Auto Recipe Formulation and Manual Recipe Formulation modes, one-click generation and rescaling of complete recipes, multi-batch handling (per-batch quantity × number of batches), "Rescale All Ingredients" proportional scaling, and saving/reloading of multiple recipes on your device;
- Machine-type and machine-capacity handling, overrun, evaporation, hot/cold process and pasteurisation calculations, storage-cabinet temperature, shelf-life and serving-size settings;
- Stabiliser, emulsifier and protein (S&E) suggestions and auto-formulation helpers;
- Hard-boundary dietary validation (e.g., dairy restriction enforcement for Sorbet and Plant-Based formulations, and pre-solve sweetness/POD feasibility alerts);
- A **Quick Check** panel on the Formulate tab giving an at-a-glance read of **Sweetness**, **Scoopability**, **Glycemic Index (GI)**, **Glycemic Load (GL)**, **Brix (°B)**, **Relative Viscosity Index (RVI)** and **Mouthfeel Index (MFI)**, with colour-coded status bands;
- A **Sensory & Physical Indices** card and bar chart reporting **Brix (°B)**, **Relative Viscosity Index** and **Mouthfeel Index** against indicative interpretation bands;
- Gauges and dashboards for water/solids balance, freezing-point depression, hardness at serving temperature, and a Freezing Point & Serving Temperature chart;
- **Optional online nutrition lookups** from public food-composition databases — USDA FoodData Central and FSANZ/AFCD (the latter also usable from a local `.xlsx`/`.csv` file you supply) — to help you create ingredient entries;
- Nutrition-label and ingredient-declaration drafting, costing, a built-in Help/Manual system, multilingual interface (18 languages), light/system/dark themes, °C/°F units, and export of recipes, labels and data (PDF, Excel/CSV, JSON) to your own device.

**The App is provided for informational and recipe-development purposes only.** It is not a substitute for:
- Professional culinary, nutritional, or dietary advice;
- Food safety certification, HACCP, or regulatory compliance guidance;
- Medical, diabetic, or allergen-safety advice.

We make reasonable efforts to keep ingredient and nutritional data plausible and current, but ingredient formulations, allergen content, and nutritional values vary between suppliers and change without notice, and we cannot guarantee completeness or accuracy. **You are solely responsible for independently verifying ingredient safety, allergen content, nutritional claims, and regulatory compliance before producing, selling, or serving any recipe generated or informed by the App.**

## 4. Calculated Metrics, Indices, Glycemic Values and Health Disclaimer

- All figures shown in the App — including **Quick Check** readings, the **Sensory & Physical Indices** card and bars, gauges, colour-coded status bands ("low / ok / high", "too hard / too soft", "Thin / Ideal / Very thick", "Poor / Fair / Good / Excellent"), serving temperature and hardness predictions, freezing-point curves, PAC/POD-type indices, cost figures, and nutrition or label panels — are **mathematical estimates derived from stored ingredient data and simplified food-science models**. They are indicative guidance for recipe development, not measured laboratory results.
- **Brix (°B)** shown by the App is an approximate dissolved-solids/sweetness index computed from recipe composition. It is **not** a refractometer or densitometer reading and may differ materially from instrument measurement of your actual mix.
- **Relative Viscosity Index (RVI)** is a dimensionless model estimate of mix thickness (roughly: 1 ≈ water-thin, 5+ ≈ very thick). It is not a rheological measurement in centipoise or any standard unit, and does not account for shear rate, temperature history, hydration time, or stabiliser interactions in your actual process.
- **Mouthfeel Index (MFI)** is a composite 0–100 score derived from fat, MSNF, total solids, modelled viscosity, overrun and freezing-point margin. It is a **model score, not a sensory panel result**, and does not predict consumer preference, flavour, aroma, or perceived quality.
- The Brix / RVI / MFI interpretation bands and the Quick Check status colours are **indicative conventions chosen for guidance only**. They are not industry standards, legal thresholds, or product-classification criteria, and a "Gelato", "Ice-cream", "Ideal" or "Excellent" reading is not a certification, grading, or claim of any kind about your product.
- **Sweetness and Scoopability** readings are relative model outputs (relative sweetening power and predicted hardness at your chosen serving temperature) and depend on your entered machine, overrun, storage and serving parameters.
- **Glycemic Index and Glycemic Load values are estimates** calculated from the sugar and carbohydrate profile of the recipe. Published GI values vary by test method, subject, ripeness, processing and matrix effects. The App's GI/GL output **must not be used to manage diabetes or any other medical condition, to make health or "low-GI"/"diabetic-friendly" marketing claims, or to replace advice from a qualified physician or dietitian.** Always consult a healthcare professional before making dietary decisions.
- Nutrition, allergen, and ingredient-declaration outputs generated for labels are drafts only. Legal food labelling in your jurisdiction may require laboratory analysis, specific rounding rules, reference intakes, allergen emphasis, and additive naming conventions that the App does not apply on your behalf.
- Overrun, machine-capacity, shelf-life, and storage-temperature guidance is indicative and depends on your actual equipment, ambient conditions, and hygiene practices.
- Auto-generated and one-click multi-recipe outputs are **starting points requiring your professional judgement and trial production**. Scaling a recipe with "Rescale All Ingredients" or across multiple batches assumes linear proportionality and does not model process, equipment or heat-transfer effects at different batch sizes.
